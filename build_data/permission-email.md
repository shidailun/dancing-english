**Subject:** Permission to make a Chinese study edition of your three novels

Dear Ms Bomboy,

I am a professor of translation at Lingnan University in Hong Kong, where I
teach Chinese-English translation. I have bought and read *The Pas de Deux*,
*The Winner* and *The Piece*, and I would like your permission to do something
with them.

I build small private reading apps for language learners — an English text with
the narration running alongside it, a tap-on-any-word dictionary giving
pronunciation and a Chinese gloss, and a Chinese rendering of each sentence that
the reader can call up when they are stuck. Your three books are the ones I would
most like to teach from: contemporary American prose, three different women
telling their own stories, and a subject — dancers and what the work costs them —
that my students will read to the end.

What I am asking for is permission to produce a Simplified Chinese translation
of the three novels, sentence by sentence, for use inside that app.

Specifically:

- The app is password-protected and not indexed. It is for me and, if you are
  willing, my translation students at Lingnan — not the public.
- Nothing is sold and nothing is advertised. There is no revenue of any kind.
- The English text is served only to someone who already has the password, and
  is stored encrypted.
- Your name and the books' titles appear on every screen; the covers are the
  app's icon.
- I will send you the translation, and I will take out anything you do not want
  in it.
- If you ever want it taken down, one email and it is gone that day.

If a Chinese edition is something you would rather keep your options open on, I
understand completely, and I will build the app without the translation — the
English, the narration and the dictionary are useful on their own.

If it would help, I am happy to sign whatever you would like me to sign.

With thanks and with real admiration for the books,

Darryl Sterk
Department of Translation, Lingnan University, Hong Kong
